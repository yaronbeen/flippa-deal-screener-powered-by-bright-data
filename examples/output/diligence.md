## Acme Metrics — manual_diligence

Source: https://flippa.com/synthetic-123-acme

### Resolve before any offer

- [ ] Verify displayed price and profit against independent financial and platform records.
- [ ] Confirm ownership, authority to sell, transfer obligations, and included assets.
- [ ] Reconcile revenue, expenses, refunds, churn, traffic sources, and concentration risks.
- [ ] Obtain qualified legal, tax, and financial review before any bid or offer.
